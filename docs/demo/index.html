Cable demo: free app for the Vitruvian Trainer+ & V-Form

Cable preview for Vitruvian V-Form & Trainer+ · unofficial

A clickable web version of the new layout with a simulated trainer, so you can try every flow without installing the app. Open the link on your phone for the real feel.

New: the first launch shows onboarding (account, optional PIN, Garmin heart rate) and an app tour with arrows. Settings → Account sets a PIN or switches account; Settings → Help replays the tour. Reset demo data to see the first launch again.

Train is now a home screen: the training you're doing (anything within 5 hours of the last exercise), live heart rate, and your oldest 1RMs to re-test. Pick an LED colour in Settings and the whole app follows it.

Analytics opens on a body map: each region is coloured by the measure and period you pick, with your best 1RM inside it per cable. Tap a region to narrow the screen to it.

Everything the classic app does has a place here: training, the 1RM test, history, analytics, profiles, trainer connection and all settings. Example lifters and workouts are pre-filled; your changes stay in this browser.

Latest: during rest go back or skip a set, end a set early, and slide the % of 1RM for the coming sets. Back leaves a workout running; a bar at the bottom resumes it. Search exercises by muscle (try “forearms”). 1RMs come only from the 1RM test or by hand: tap one in Analytics to correct it.
