Cable: free app for the Vitruvian Trainer+ & V-Form

Cable is a free, open-source Android app for the Vitruvian Trainer+ and V-Form home gym. It talks to the trainer directly over Bluetooth: no subscription, no ads, no tracking and no cloud account.

  • Up to 4 lifters per session, each with their own 1RM and PIN
  • Loads as a % of your 1RM, with estimated 1RMs for 362 exercises
  • Analytics with a muscle body map
  • Offline first, with backups to a single JSON file
