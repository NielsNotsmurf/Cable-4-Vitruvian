Cable privacy policy

Applies to the Cable Android app (Google Play and GitHub) and the Cable web app. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

Short version: Cable has no accounts and no servers. Everything you enter or record stays on your own device. The app never sells, shares or uploads your workouts, profiles or heart rate.

Who we are

Cable is a free, open-source hobby project by Niels, a private individual in the Netherlands. It is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with Vitruvian. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.

What is stored, and where

DataWhyWhere
Lifter profiles (name, colour, optional PIN, body weight, 1RMs)To train several people on one trainerOnly on your device
Workouts, sets, reps, loads, routines, personal recordsHistory, progress and analyticsOnly on your device
Heart rate from a watch or chest strap you pairShown live during a set and kept with that setOnly on your device
Settings (units, colours, layout)To remember your choicesOnly on your device

Backups are files you create yourself (Download/Cable on Android, a downloaded file on the web). You decide where they go.

Bluetooth and location

Cable uses Bluetooth to connect to the Vitruvian trainer and, if you choose, a heart rate sensor. On Android 12 and newer it asks only for "Nearby devices" and never uses your location. Android 11 and older require the location permission for any Bluetooth scan; Cable uses it only to find the trainer and never reads or stores where you are.

Heart rate (health data)

Heart rate is read directly from the sensor you pair, only while the app is open, and is stored only on your device. It is not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, and not uploaded. Cable is not a medical device.

Network

The Android app connects to the internet only to download updates of the public exercise library from GitHub. No personal or workout data is sent with it. Links you tap (feedback, this page) open in your browser.

Web app: anonymous usage counts

The hosted web app counts a few anonymous events with GoatCounter: app opened (installed or in the browser), device type (phone, tablet, desktop), browser name, workout finished, and whether the trainer connection worked. It uses no cookies, no identifiers and nothing from your workouts or profiles. It is off when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, and you can turn it off in Settings. The Android app has no analytics at all.

No ads, no selling, no sharing

Cable shows no ads, contains no advertising or tracking SDKs, and never sells or shares data with third parties.

Children

Cable is meant for adults using strength training equipment and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Everything lives on your device, so you are in full control. In the app: Settings, Backup and restore, delete all workouts, or delete a profile in Profiles. To remove everything at once: uninstall the Android app (or clear its storage), or clear the site data for the web app in your browser. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data is held about you and have it deleted. Because Cable holds no data about you outside your own device, there is nothing for us to hand over or delete, but feel free to ask on GitHub.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the app's open-source repository.
